When you submit an audit request, I collect the following information:

The information you submit is used solely to:

By submitting an audit request, you agree to receive a response to your inquiry via email. You may opt out of any further communication at any time by replying "unsubscribe" to any email.

I retain your contact information only as long as necessary to fulfil the service or for a reasonable period following the conclusion of any business relationship. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting me directly.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

7. Security

This site uses HTTPS encryption. Form data is transmitted securely. I take reasonable precautions to protect your information, though no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

I may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.
